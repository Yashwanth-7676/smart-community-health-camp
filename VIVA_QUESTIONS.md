# Viva Questions and Expected Answers

## 1. What is the purpose of this project?
This project demonstrates how a community health camp can manage patient registration, queue flow, staff roles, medicines, and reporting in a fictional educational environment.

## 2. Why is it a demo instead of a real healthcare system?
Because it is built with browser localStorage and no backend authentication or secure database. It intentionally avoids real health information.

## 3. How does role-based access work?
Different user roles such as Administrator, Camp Organizer, Doctor, Nurse, Volunteer, and Pharmacist are assigned different portal tabs and permissions.

## 4. What happens to patient data?
Patient data remains in the browser as local demo records only. No real patient data should be entered or stored.

## 5. Why is there an AI assistant if it is not a real medical AI?
The AI is a demo administrative assistant that helps summarize operational data, explain queue status, and generate educational content. It is restricted from diagnosis and prescribing.

## 6. What are the major features of the app?
The app includes login, dashboard, camp workflow, patient registration, queue management, inventory, reports, theme settings, and AI safety boundaries.

## 7. What is the difference between the workflow and the dashboard?
The dashboard summarizes the current camp state, while the workflow guides users step by step through the operational sequence of a camp visit.

## 8. What is the significance of safety and privacy documentation?
It clarifies the limits of the application and ensures the project is presented as an educational prototype rather than a medical product.

## 9. What are the major limitations?
There is no real backend, no secure authentication, no production database, and no external clinical integration.

## 10. What is planned for the future version?
A secure backend, real authentication, cloud sync, improved reporting, and more robust mobile and deployment support would be required for real-world use.

## Short oral explanation
This project is a browser-based educational health camp management prototype that models a real operational flow in a safe and simplified way without using real patient information.
