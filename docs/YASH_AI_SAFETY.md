# Yash AI Safety

Yash AI provides administrative and educational assistance only. It does not diagnose, prescribe, or replace qualified healthcare professionals.

## Allowed
- Aggregate camp and queue summaries
- Fictional inventory totals
- Operational reports and briefings
- Camp planning checklists
- Poster and health-education drafts
- Translation drafts requiring human review
- Data-quality suggestions that never modify records
- Project presentation and viva support

## Refused
- Diagnosis or disease prediction
- Medicine or treatment recommendations
- Emergency treatment instructions
- Individual referral decisions
- Individual risk prediction
- Unnecessary access to personal or clinical data

## Human control
Yash AI never changes records silently and does not make autonomous clinical decisions. A qualified professional or responsible organizer must review health-related content and all operational suggestions.

## Demo transparency
The interface clearly identifies Demo Mode, uses local rule-based responses, shows a data preview, and labels responses as generated from fictional SmartCare Camp demo data. No external provider receives data.
